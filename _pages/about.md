I am a PhD candidate in Finance and Accounting at the Indian Institute of Management Bodh Gaya, currently awaiting my final defence. My research lies at the intersection of climate finance, corporate finance, and investor sentiment. I study how capital markets respond to climate regulation and green financing decisions, and how investor sentiment transmits across asset classes and national markets. I am also interested in household finance and the behavioural foundations of individual financial decisions.

My work has appeared in *Applied Economics* and *The Oxford Handbook of Social Welfare in the Global South*, and two of my papers are under revision at the *International Review of Financial Analysis*. I have presented my research at the Salata Institute workshop on the Financial Economics of Climate and Sustainability at Harvard University, the Global Finance Conference, the India Responsible Capital Conference at IIM Ahmedabad, and the Pan IIT International Conference at IIT Madras.

During my doctoral studies, I was a Visiting Scholar at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. I hold an M.Com in Finance and Accounting from the University of Burdwan, where I was awarded the University Gold Medal.

**Research interests:** Climate Finance, Corporate Finance, Investor Sentiment, Household Finance
